Introduction

Welcome to Velvet Whisk. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you choose to share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when interacting with Velvet Whisk through our website, enquiry forms and communication channels.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Velvet Whisk, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Cake preferences and order requirements.
  • Celebration dates and occasion details that you choose to provide.
  • Messages or other information you submit through enquiry forms.

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to Velvet Whisk may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and questions.
  • Discuss cake orders and custom cake requirements.
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry or order.
  • Provide information about our products and services when appropriate.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.

Information Sharing

Velvet Whisk does not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to operate our services, respond to your request, comply with applicable legal requirements, or protect our rights and customers.

Cookies

Our website may use basic browser technologies such as cookies or similar mechanisms to improve website functionality and user experience.

Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies through its settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning the personal information you provide, including requesting access, correction or deletion where applicable.

If you have a privacy-related request, please contact us using the details provided below.

Changes to This Policy

Velvet Whisk may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact Velvet Whisk.
